# 📊 Marketing Funnel & Customer Conversion Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing marketing funnel data to understand customer conversion behavior and identify major drop-offs in the customer journey. The dashboard provides insights into conversion trends, channel performance, and campaign effectiveness to help improve lead-to-customer conversion rates.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer conversion funnel
- Identify major conversion drop-offs
- Evaluate channel and campaign performance
- Measure conversion rate
- Generate actionable business insights

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI & Python
- Excel
- Data Visualization

---

## 📊 Key Metrics
- Total Customers: 86K
- Converted Customers: 10K
- Conversion Rate: 11.49%

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 📉 Funnel Analysis
- A significant drop-off is observed between total customers and converted customers.
- Only 11.49% of customers completed conversion.

### 📅 Monthly Conversion Analysis
- May recorded the highest customer conversions.
- Conversion performance varied across different months.

### 📞 Contact Method Performance
- Cellular contact generated the highest conversions.
- Telephone and unknown channels showed lower performance.

### 📣 Campaign Performance
- Customer engagement differed across campaigns.
- Some campaigns performed significantly better than others.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- Customer Conversion Funnel
- Month-wise Conversion Analysis
- Contact Method Analysis
- Campaign Performance Analysis
- Conversion Distribution Overview

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Focus more on cellular-based marketing campaigns.
- Improve lead nurturing strategies to reduce funnel drop-offs.
- Optimize low-performing campaigns.
- Analyze successful strategies from high-conversion months.

---

## 📌 Conclusion
This project helps identify conversion bottlenecks and marketing performance gaps through funnel analysis and interactive visualizations. The insights can support better decision-making and improve overall customer conversion rates.

---

## 📎 Deliverable
Interactive Funnel Performance Dashboard with actionable business insights and conversion optimization recommendations.
